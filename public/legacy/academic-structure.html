Graduate Designations, Career Pathways & the APGDM

The Albalagh Professional Graduate Development Model

Every Professional Diploma student follows the same structured pathway — combining academic excellence, practical competence, workplace experience, and formal conferment of an institutional Graduate Designation.

Step 01

Admission

Step 02

Professional Diploma Programme

Three semesters over one year.

Step 03

Lectures, Tutorials & Practical Classes

Continuous assessment, projects, and research.

Step 04

Compulsory Industrial Attachment / Internship

Six weeks of workplace-based learning.

Step 05

Academic Board Approval

Step 06

Official Statement of Results

Step 07

AIPS Professional Competency and Practice Programme

Five weeks with the Albalagh Institute of Professional Studies.

Step 08

Professional Competency Assessment

Step 09

Online Convocation

Step 10

Award of the Professional Diploma Certificate

Step 11

Award of the Professional Practice Certificate (AIPS)

Step 12

Formal Conferment of the Institutional Graduate Designation

Step 13

Employment, Entrepreneurship & Professional Practice

Further studies and lifelong learning continue beyond graduation.

Upon successfully completing the five-week Professional Competency and Practice Programme and meeting all graduation requirements, graduates are formally conferred with their approved Albalagh institutional Graduate Designation during the graduation process.

Integration Across All Schools

One model, tailored by discipline

Every School follows the same graduate development model — internships, practical activities, projects, and professional assessments are tailored to each discipline.

School of Islamic Sciences

Practical components centre on applied scholarship, community teaching exposure, and research writing under faculty supervision.

School of Media, Journalism and Digital Communication

Internships take place in newsrooms and media production settings, building a real portfolio of published or produced work.

School of Artificial Intelligence, Innovation and Technology

Practical work centres on applied technical projects, data or software builds, and structured innovation labs.

School of Business, Entrepreneurship and Financial Management

Attachments take place in business, financial, or marketing settings, with a venture-building or financial project component.

Self-Employment & Entrepreneurship

Building independent careers

Beyond formal employment, graduates are equipped to establish independent careers through consultancy, freelancing, and discipline-specific entrepreneurial ventures — from educational services and media production to software solutions, AI services, and accounting practice where appropriate.

Important Notice: Graduate Designations are institutional recognitions awarded by Albalagh Global.
The Professional Practice Certificate issued by AIPS is an institutional certification recognising successful completion of the College's competency programme.
Neither the Graduate Designation nor the Professional Practice Certificate constitutes a statutory licence or protected professional title.
Professional practice, regulated occupations, licensing, and employment remain subject to applicable laws, employer requirements, and the regulations of relevant professional bodies.
