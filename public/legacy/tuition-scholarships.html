Tuition Philosophy

Access should never be the barrier

Albalagh prices its programmes to remain within reach of serious students, while sustaining the quality of teaching, technology, and support every student deserves.

We are building a regional tuition framework and scholarship programme precisely because a single flat global rate would exclude many capable students — and a college that prices out its own mission isn't serving anyone.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Seven categories of support, in development

Albalagh is building a structured scholarship programme. Availability, amounts, and eligibility for each category are subject to institutional approval and confirmed at the time applications open.

Merit Scholarships

For outstanding academic achievement at application.

Need-Based Scholarships

For applicants demonstrating genuine financial need.

Sponsored Scholarships

Funded by partner organisations and sponsors.

Institutional Scholarships

Funded directly by the College.

Alumni Scholarships

Supported by Albalagh graduates giving back.

Endowment Scholarships

Funded by long-term institutional endowment.

Special Intervention Scholarships

For exceptional individual circumstances.

More categories may be added as the programme grows.

International Students

Studying at Albalagh from anywhere

Because Albalagh is delivered fully online, international students join without visa or relocation requirements tied to study — tuition category and currency handling are the main considerations, both covered by the regional tuition framework above.

International payment methods and currency conversion details will be confirmed as the online payment gateway is finalised.
