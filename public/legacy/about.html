About The College

Knowledge and character, built for the modern world

Albalagh Global — Islamic Sciences and Modern Civilization is an online institution dedicated to producing graduates who are scholarly, capable, and principled.

Who We Are

An institution built at the intersection of tradition and progress

Albalagh exists to close a gap many students face: the choice between deep Islamic scholarship and relevant modern skills. We reject that choice entirely.

Across four schools — Islamic Sciences, Digital Technology & AI, Business & Entrepreneurship, and Professional Freelancing & Digital Careers — students build a foundation in both sacred knowledge and practical capability, guided by faculty committed to rigorous, honest instruction.

We are an online-first institution by design, not by necessity — built to reach committed learners wherever they are, without compromising academic standard.

Our Story

Founded on a simple conviction

Albalagh was founded on 6 January 2024 out of a conviction that Islamic education deserved the same digital infrastructure, design quality, and institutional seriousness as any leading modern university.

What began as a single vision has grown into a structured college of four schools and five research centres, built deliberately and incrementally rather than announced with claims it could not yet stand behind.

Our aspiration is straightforward: to serve students locally and internationally who want an education that is honest about where it is today, and clear about where it is going.
