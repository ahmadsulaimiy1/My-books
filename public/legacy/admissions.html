Who Can Apply

Six admission routes, one application

Route 01

Standard Secondary Qualifications

  • WAEC
  • NECO
  • GCE
  • NABTEB
  • NBAIS
  • International Secondary Certificates
  • Other recognised equivalents
Route 02

Islamic Education Route

  • Thanawiyyah
  • Senior Arabic & Islamic Studies Certificates
  • Recognised Islamic Institutions
  • NBAIS Senior Qualifications
Route 03

Awaiting Results

Applicants awaiting results may apply now. Provisional Admission may be granted, subject to the qualifying result being submitted and verified before graduation or by the institutional deadline.

Route 04

Certificate Progression

Applicants holding relevant certificates from Albalagh, recognised colleges, professional training centres, technical institutions, or Islamic institutions may apply following curriculum evaluation.

Route 05

Mature Learner Route

Applicants without formal secondary qualifications but with professional experience, vocational competence, Islamic scholarship, workplace experience, community leadership, or demonstrated academic ability may apply through institutional assessment.

Route 06

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

Applicants may demonstrate competence through portfolio, interview, challenge examination, professional experience, community learning, workplace learning, or Islamic study, where applicable.
