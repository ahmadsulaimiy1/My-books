Introduction

Built as infrastructure, not decoration

Al-Balagh's Digital Campus is the system through which every student studies, every lecturer teaches, and every administrator manages the institution — not a separate "online option" bolted onto a physical college.

This page previews that system honestly: what exists today, what's actively being built, and what's planned for the future. Nothing here claims to be live unless it is.
