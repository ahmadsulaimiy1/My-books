Institutional Governance

Leadership, accountability, and integrity are not abstractions here — they are structured into how Albalagh is governed, at every level, from the Board of Trustees to the classroom.

Governance Philosophy

Structure in service of trust

Albalagh is governed through clearly defined structures designed to promote responsible leadership, academic freedom, accountability, and transparency at every level.

No single office holds unchecked authority. Strategic direction, policy-making, academic standards, and daily administration are deliberately separated across distinct bodies — each accountable to the next.

Institutional Objects

The formal Objects of the College

The following Objects are formally defined in the constitutional and legal instruments establishing Albalagh Global, and describe the purposes for which the College is established and operated.

  1. To establish, own, manage, and operate educational institutions including colleges, institutes, academies, schools, learning centres, and universities for the promotion of Islamic sciences, modern sciences, technology, vocational education, professional development, and lifelong learning.
  2. To provide physical, online, distance, hybrid, and virtual education, training, certification, diploma, professional development programmes, seminars, workshops, conferences, and research activities.
  3. To promote the teaching and learning of the Holy Qur'an, Hadith, Fiqh, Aqeedah, Arabic Language, Islamic Civilization, Comparative Religion, Law, Humanities, Social Sciences, Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, Business, Entrepreneurship, Digital Skills, and other academic and professional disciplines.
  4. To establish libraries, research centres, innovation hubs, laboratories, digital learning platforms, publishing houses, and educational technology services.
  5. To develop educational software, websites, mobile applications, e-learning platforms, digital libraries, multimedia educational content, and virtual classrooms.
  6. To award certificates, diplomas, professional certifications, and other educational qualifications as permitted by applicable laws and regulations.
  7. To collaborate with educational institutions, universities, government agencies, international organisations, professional bodies, charitable organisations, and development partners within and outside Nigeria.
  8. To organise educational competitions, public lectures, conferences, exhibitions, outreach programmes, scholarship schemes, mentorship programmes, leadership training, and community development initiatives.
  9. To publish, print, translate, distribute, and sell books, journals, magazines, research papers, teaching materials, digital publications, and other educational resources.
  10. To provide consultancy, educational advisory services, curriculum development, institutional management, teacher training, quality assurance, and capacity-building services.
  11. To acquire, lease, build, equip, and maintain land, buildings, campuses, hostels, libraries, laboratories, offices, and other facilities necessary for achieving the objectives of the College.
  12. To receive grants, donations, endowments, gifts, subscriptions, and other lawful sources of funding, and to apply such funds solely towards the attainment of the College's objectives.
  13. To undertake all lawful activities that are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the foregoing objectives in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our governance

Albalagh is governed through a structured hierarchy led by the Board of Trustees, the Governing Council, the Academic Board, and the Management Board, supported by standing committees.

The Governing Council is the highest policy-making body, approving institutional policies, annual budgets, and strategic development, while overseeing governance and institutional performance.

The Academic Board is the highest academic authority, approving programmes, curricula, and examination regulations, and recommending students for academic awards in accordance with the College's Academic Regulations.

Academic quality is maintained through the Academic Board, the Quality Assurance Committee, and continuous review processes aligned with international best practice.

Accountability is ensured through defined governance roles, committee oversight, financial audit processes, and transparent institutional reporting.
