The Graduate Journey

The Albalagh Professional Graduate Development Model (APGDM)

Every Professional Diploma student follows this structured pathway — combining academic excellence, practical competence, ethical values, workplace experience, leadership, innovation, entrepreneurship, and career readiness.

Upon successfully completing the five-week Professional Competency and Practice Programme and meeting all graduation requirements, graduates are formally conferred with their approved Albalagh institutional Graduate Designation during the graduation process.
Integration Across All Schools

One model, tailored to each discipline

Every School follows the same graduate development model, while internships, practical activities, projects, competency training, and professional assessments are tailored to the student's discipline.

School of Islamic Sciences

Practical components centre on applied scholarship — teaching practice, community guidance placements, and structured research under faculty supervision.

School of Media, Journalism and Digital Communication

Internships take place in newsrooms and production environments, with competency assessment built around real published or produced work.

School of Artificial Intelligence, Innovation and Technology

Practical work centres on project-based development and applied technical placements, assessed through working builds and technical review.

School of Business, Entrepreneurship and Financial Management

Attachment placements span business, financial, and marketing environments, with competency assessed through applied case work and venture exercises.

Career Opportunities & Employment Pathways

Where Albalagh graduates may take their designation

Career opportunities and sectors are indicative and non-exhaustive. Albalagh does not guarantee employment; outcomes depend on the graduate's qualifications, experience, and employer requirements.
