Albalagh Institute of Professional Studies

Where academic learning becomes professional practice

The Albalagh Institute of Professional Studies (AIPS) is the professional development and competency assessment arm of Albalagh Global. The Institute is established to bridge the gap between academic learning and professional practice by equipping graduates with practical skills, ethical values, workplace competence, leadership abilities, and entrepreneurial capacity before the award of their final graduation documents.

Admission into the Institute

A compulsory step for every Diploma graduate

Admission into the Institute is compulsory for all students enrolled in Diploma Programmes of Albalagh Global, following successful completion of their academic studies.

Upon completion of the Diploma Programme, students receive an Official Statement of Results and proceed to the Institute for the Professional Competency and Practice Programme (PCPP).

Duration
Five (5) Weeks

Every Diploma student also completes a compulsory six-week Industrial Attachment / Internship during the Diploma Programme itself, before reaching AIPS.

Programme Structure

Five weeks, from foundation to certification

The PCPP is delivered across five structured weeks, each building on the last.

Week One — Professional Foundation

Professional Ethics and Conduct · Leadership Development · Workplace Communication · Emotional Intelligence · Professional Discipline

Week Two — Practical Skills Development

Practical Field Assignments · Industry-Based Case Studies · Team Collaboration · Workplace Problem Solving · Professional Documentation

Week Three — Innovation and Professional Practice

Independent Practical Project · Portfolio Development · Digital Competency · Innovation and Creativity · Personal Branding

Week Four — Professional Assessment

Technical Report Writing · Project Presentation · Oral Defence (Viva Voce) · Professional Interview · Professional Ethics Assessment

Week Five — Career and Competency Certification

Final Competency Assessment · Career Development · Entrepreneurship and Business Start-up · Employment Readiness · Exit Interview · Professional Membership Orientation

Assessment Requirements

What every participant must complete

Every participant must successfully complete each of the following:

  • Practical Field Assignment
  • Professional Project
  • Portfolio Assessment
  • Technical Report
  • Oral Defence (Viva Voce)
  • Professional Ethics Assessment
  • Final Competency Assessment
Certification

What graduates receive

Graduates who successfully complete the Programme receive:

  • Official Diploma Certificate from Albalagh Global
  • Professional Practice Certificate from the Albalagh Institute of Professional Studies (AIPS)
  • Professional Competency Record
Institutional Status

The Professional Practice Certificate issued by AIPS is an institutional certification recognising successful completion of the College's professional competency programme. It is distinct from any statutory professional licence or government-issued professional qualification.

Governance & Administrative Structure

How AIPS is governed

The Albalagh Institute of Professional Studies (AIPS) is the official Professional Education, Competency Development, Assessment, and Certification Institute of Albalagh Global. The Institute operates under the authority of the Governing Council and Academic Board of the College and is responsible for coordinating the Professional Competency and Practice Programme for Diploma graduates.

Institute Officers

Roles that keep AIPS running

Institute Secretary

The Institute's Chief Administrative Officer — coordinates administrative activities, maintains official Institute records, prepares meetings and correspondence, coordinates student registration into the Institute, processes certification documentation, and liaises with the College Registrar.

Coordinator of Professional Training

Plans the Professional Practice Programme, schedules lectures and workshops, and coordinates facilitators, trainers, and student participation.

Coordinator of Assessments and Certification

Manages examinations and practical assessments, coordinates viva voce sessions, supervises portfolio evaluations, and prepares certification recommendations.

Coordinator of Industrial Partnership and Internship

Develops relationships with industries and organisations, coordinates internship placements, and supervises and evaluates internship reports and workplace performance.

Professional Examinations Officer

Manages professional examinations, maintains examination integrity, coordinates assessment logistics, and prepares examination results.

Certification and Records Officer

Maintains certification records, issues Professional Practice Certificates, and manages graduate verification and alumni professional records.

Quality Assurance Officer

Monitors programme quality, evaluates teaching and assessment standards, and reviews professional training outcomes for continuous improvement.

The following College officers provide institutional support to the Institute: Registrar, Bursar, Librarian, the ICT Unit, the Quality Assurance Directorate, the Legal and Compliance Office, and the Student Affairs Division.
