SubjectThe completion of the work, and what it owes to you

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Assalāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh,

Umm Abdillah,

My love, it comes to you before anyone else. Of the five thousand pages I have just finished, none says this: nothing I finish will ever mean to me what you mean.

Go on with your studies and your work; let nothing make them smaller, least of all me. Rise, and I will stand for you first. Let us walk as one, nothing asked of you that is not asked of me too.

May Allah elevate you, and fulfil for us what we hope for.

With love and respect,
Your Habeeb,

Ahmad
