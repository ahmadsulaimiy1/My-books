## 1. SCHOLARLY

- **Review tags: none left in any volume.**
  - «استنباط تربوي»: 203 tags lifted into the author's voice.
  - Every other tag was settled on the printed edition in research batches: B1–B2, D1–D2, H1–H3, P, M1–M2, T1–T2 and V1R.
  - The record of how each tag was settled is in `التحقيق/وسوم-المجلدات-٢-١١.md` and `التحقيق/وسوم-المجلد-الأول.md`.
- **Hadith audit (V2–V11).** 116 hadith and athar quoted with an attribution were matched on the printed Ṣaḥīḥs and Sunan: Sulṭāniyya with ʿAbd al-Bāqī's numbers, ʿAbd al-Bāqī's Muslim, Muḥyī al-Dīn's Abū Dāwūd, and the Ḥalabī al-Tirmidhī.
  - Every one now carries a takhrīj note: number, kitāb and bāb as printed, the grading in the grader's own words, and «واللفظ لـ…» where the wording is one collection's.
  - 13 wordings were corrected and 3 claims fixed, for example: «الدالّ على الخير كفاعله» is al-Tirmidhī's, not Muslim's; «لك صدقة»; «ثم أبوك»; «فاستقم»; «الصلح جائز».
- **Substantive corrections from the print.** Among them:
  - al-Shāfiʿī's sayings («لم أبال», not a wish);
  - «رأيي صواب يحتمل الخطأ» is not established for any imam;
  - the «حروف الصفير» sentence is not in Sībawayh;
  - the dhalāqa letters are al-Khalīl's;
  - the modern ḍād is a stop at the incisor roots, now aligned in V2, the V11 card and appendix C;
  - the ʿayn's rarity is worldwide, not "outside Semitic";
  - the V8 story of Ibn ʿAbbās follows Bukhārī 4970;
  - khuṭbat al-ḥāja is shown as a composite of two narrations;
  - Ibn Khaldūn's exception on first-language habits;
  - Carnegie 1936 on praise;
  - Toulmin's sixth element;
  - STAR in three parts;
  - Mehrabian's formula applies to faces and feelings only.
- **Modern sources.** Every modern-analysis statement is now either tied to a source seen with its page, or given bibliographic data without a page, or recast in the author's voice with vague collective attributions removed. Specialist questions are settled from the literature or stated as named disagreements.
- **Ledger** (`سجل-النقول`): 413 rows.
  - 384 verified, 13 verified with a variant, and 4 of uncertain attribution (stated as such in the text).
  - 12 incomplete citations remain.
  - Evidence images: `التحقيق/مصوّرات/` (Volume 1) and `مصوّرات/المجلدات-٢-١١/` (607 crops).
- **Bibliographies.**
  - Each of V1–V10 closes on a thabat generated from the sources base.
  - V11 closes on the series thabat (88 Arabic and 45 foreign entries, each with the volumes that cite it).
  - The old hand-written V11 list is archived.
- **Indexes.** Every volume has the six indexes of the Bible, ch. 111 §4: verses, hadith and athar, poetry, people, terms and sources, with page numbers.

## 2. EDITORIAL

- Production language was removed from the printed text:
  - «يُعرض على مختص» wording in V2 and in V11 appendix B;
  - a tag nested inside an italic aside in V10, now caught by preflight;
  - hedges such as «فيما يُذكر».
- Terms and names were unified: Grice's «قاعدة الكمّ»; Corder's title «Learner's», as printed.
- «في هذا السياق» was removed where the Bible bans it.
- **Left for the author (style only, flagged «للمراجعة»):** «في هذا الفصل» appears twice or more in 12 lesson files. It was not reworded mechanically. «مفتاح النجاح» remains only where it is quoted as a cliché to avoid (V4 ch. 8).

## 3. DESIGN

- **Collapsed columns fixed.** A graded model speech (①②③) or a ✘/✔ example line that ran on to the next page continued there one word to the line, because its body fell into the mark's column. This affected about 110 pages in ten volumes. The body now keeps its column. Page counts fell accordingly (V9: 363 → 337). Preflight now fails any page set that way.
- **Font fallbacks fixed.**
  - The indexes' Latin entries now fall back to the text face for their Arabic glosses.
  - The Bible draws ▣ as a vector mark and sets ↑ and ↓ in Plex.
  - IPA «ˤ» (in no book face) was dropped from V11 appendix C.
- **Remaining page-by-page items (review, not failure).** Short pages inside chapters (1–10 per volume) and a few headings near a page foot are listed in each `الإخراج/فحص-المجلد-*.md`. The two V8 "headings at page foot" were checked by eye and are not stranded headings. Spreads for the visual pass are in `الإخراج/*_Spreads.pdf`.
