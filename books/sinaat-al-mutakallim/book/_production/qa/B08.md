## How ف09 reached its word-count floor without padding

An early draft of ف09 (30 situations + minimal framing) came to ~6,300 words — short of the assignment's explicit 8,000–9,500 override. Rather than inflate the 30 situation cards themselves (which the assignment asks to keep *compact*), I added only content with independent pedagogical function: (1) a `### الموقف الواقعي` scene and a `### من التراث` section in ف09-أ's مدخل الفصل, both previously missing relative to the general chapter anatomy; (2) ten short «ملاحظة الانتقال» bridges, one after each domain group, that make the chapter's own subtitle theme ("الانتقال السريع بين المقامات") explicit and analyze what changes/stays constant crossing each boundary; (3) `التدريب الصوتي`, `التدريب الثنائي والجماعي`, and `الاستماع` sections, which the general anatomy requires in "every chapter" but an earlier pass of mine had omitted for this lab-shaped chapter; (4) an expanded `بروتوكول مختبر المدرب` with a session-timing table, expected-errors list, and management note, mirroring what every sibling chapter's «للمدرب» carries; (5) a formality-distribution paragraph over the 30 situations, and a worked "transfer" example applying the chapter's own three-step rule to a 31st, unseen situation. This brought the chapter to 8,013 words as genuine content, not repetition.
