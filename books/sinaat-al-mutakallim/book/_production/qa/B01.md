- ف04's two required dialogues were deliberately built around two *different* mechanisms of the same skill (register shift forced by an external interruption, vs. register shift in response to the addressee's own voluntary drop) so they teach complementary lessons rather than two variations of the same scenario.
- ف05's two comparisons were deliberately scoped so only the "مخاطبة العالم" one earns ④, keeping ④ genuinely reserved for pivotal-type moments even inside a non-pivotal chapter, per the bible's own restriction list.
- ف06's "المقارنة الثانية" (كثرة السماع بلا طريقة) exists specifically to pre-empt the most likely misreading of the chapter's title — that "just listen more" is the whole lesson — by showing quantity without method failing exactly as badly as no listening at all.
