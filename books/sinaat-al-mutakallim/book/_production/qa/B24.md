## Design notes / continuity choices

- ف03's title and five-letter scope («الضاد، الظاء، القاف، العين، الحاء») was taken verbatim from both the book map (`02-خريطة-الكتاب.md`, القسم الثالث عشر، بند ٣) and the bab opener's «فصول الباب» table, which agree exactly.
- ف04's scope (نحوي، صرفي، معجمي، تركيبي) likewise matches the map's بند ٤ exactly. I ordered the four sub-categories نحوي → صرفي → معجمي → تركيبي (map order) and gave grammar the largest share (16 of 32 cards) since it is the largest and most heterogeneous category and the one most learners actively produce errors in during spontaneous speech.
- To avoid inventing non-words, every minimal-pair example in ف03 and every lexical-confusion example in ف04 uses two real, independently attested Arabic words (e.g. حَظّ/حَزّ, نَظِير/نَضِير, أَثَر/إِثْر, العالَم/العالِم) rather than a real word paired with an invented one.
- Card ٢١ in ف04-ب (الفعل الأجوف مسندًا إلى ضمير المتكلم) required an explicit disambiguating note inside its "التعليل" field, because «قالت» is independently correct (فعل مسند إلى ضمير مستتر «هي»، بتاء التأنيث الساكنة) and I did not want the card to read as banning a correct form — the card is scoped precisely to إسناد الفعل الأجوف إلى تاء الفاعل المتحركة (أنا/أنتَ/نحن), not to «قالت» in general.
- Did not touch ف02, ف05, ف06, ف07, or ف08 — read-only, for format reference.
