- **ف06 (أثر اللغات المحلية) and ف05 (الترجمة الحرفية من الإنجليزية)** are complementary by design, not overlapping: ف06 is about L1/local-language transfer into sentence order and address forms (no English involved), ف05 is specifically English-medium calque at the word/phrase level (give/take/make/do, prepositions, passive-with-تمّ, dummy pronouns, idioms, tense, compound-noun order) — I skimmed ف06 first to confirm it never touches these categories before committing to them for ف05.
- **ف07 (الحشو والتكرار والغموض)** already owns "حشو" as a concept; ف09's lesson 2 (متى تطيل) explicitly cross-references it with a dedicated "الفرق بين الإطناب المحمود والحشو المذموم" comparison table, rather than re-teaching حشو from scratch, so a reader who did ف07 first sees ف09 build on it, not repeat it.
- **Cross-chapter continuity of examples:** the ف08-ج closing dialogue (بلال يستشير الشيخ عبد الله قبل خطبته) deliberately packs one error from each of ف08's own two lessons (غريب متكلف؛ إيحاء جارح؛ سجل أكاديمي في غير موضعه) *and* one direct ترجمة حرفية echo of ف05 («خذوا نظرة على أنفسكم» → «فلننظر في أنفسنا»), sending the reader back to ف05 rather than re-explaining it. ف04's intro scenario (يوسف ينسّق مسابقة حفظ القرآن) is reused as the chapter's own formal graded comparison later in the same file instead of inventing a second, unrelated scene.
